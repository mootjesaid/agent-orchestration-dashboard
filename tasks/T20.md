## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Do not replay ambiguous mutating commands or stale approvals.
- [ ] Missing history, external edits or mismatched HEAD become visible blocked states with choices.
- [ ] Pause/cancel preserve work; verify SQLite-aware backup/restore procedure.
